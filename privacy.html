Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Your photos stay on your Mac. Lightbox has no account and no cloud, and it never uploads photos, file names, folder paths, locations, camera details or edits. It talks to the internet for two small things, both described below, and both can be turned off.

Update checks

Each time it opens, and every hour while it stays open, Lightbox downloads a short list of available versions from this website. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address and the Lightbox version to the website's host. Turn this off in Lightbox › Settings › Check for updates automatically; Lightbox › Check for Updates… still checks when you ask.

Anonymous usage counts

To know whether anyone uses Lightbox, and which macOS versions matter, it sends two kinds of anonymous signal to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in the EU:

  • “Launched”, when Lightbox opens.
  • “Exported”, when an export finishes, with the number of photos it contained.

Each signal carries the Lightbox version and build, the macOS version, and a random identifier created when Lightbox is first opened. The identifier is hashed on your Mac before it is sent and hashed again by TelemetryDeck, so it counts installations without identifying anyone. TelemetryDeck doesn't store IP addresses. Nothing else is sent: no photos, names, paths, settings or edit details.

Turn this off in Lightbox › Settings › Share anonymous usage counts. Nothing is sent afterwards.

Problem reports

Help › Report a Problem… opens a new email in your own mail app with the Lightbox version, macOS version and Mac model filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself, and you can remove anything you'd rather not share. Crash reports are only shared if you attach one.

Your edits and settings

Edits and favourites are saved in hidden .lightbox folders beside your photos. Settings live in ~/Library/Preferences/app.lightbox.mac.plist, and thumbnails and previews in ~/Library/Caches/Lightbox. None of it leaves your Mac.

This website

The site uses no cookies, trackers or analytics. Your light or dark choice is remembered in your browser's local storage. The host that serves the pages may keep standard access logs, such as IP addresses, under its own policy.
